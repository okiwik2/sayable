Sayable privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026. Applies to the Sayable app for Android, the Sayable website and Sayable Lite.

In short: Sayable has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Everything you make in Sayable stays on your device. We don't collect, receive or sell any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your words, boards, messages, settings, emergency details and contacts, and any photos or videos you add are stored only on your device, inside Sayable. We can't see them. They leave your device only if you choose to export a backup or share something, and then they go only where you send them.

On Android, if you have turned on your device's own backup to your Google account, Android may include Sayable's data in that backup. That backup is between you and Google; we can't see it.

Location

If you turn on the safety features and tap the button to show your location on the emergency screen, Sayable asks your device for its location and shows it on the screen so you can show it to someone. It is not saved and is not sent anywhere.

Camera, photos and videos

If you take or choose a photo for a word, or record a sign video, it is stored on your device only. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo or video.

When Sayable uses the internet

  • Sign videos: sign-language videos are downloaded from the NZSL dictionary (nzsl.nz) and saved on your device.
  • Symbol search: if you search for a new picture for a word, the search words are sent to ARASAAC to find matching pictures.
  • The website: the Sayable website and Sayable Lite are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Updates: the Android app downloaded from the Sayable website (not Google Play) checks GitHub for a newer version.

These services receive only what is needed to answer the request. Sayable sends them nothing about you.

Speech

Sayable speaks using your device's own text-to-speech voices. Most voices work on the device. Some online voices offered by your device or browser may send the text to the voice provider (for example Google or Apple) under their privacy policy.

Children

Sayable may be used by children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and has no ads, in-app purchases or chat.

Deleting your information

Because everything is on your device, you can delete it at any time by removing Sayable or clearing its data (on a website: clearing the site's data in your browser).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
